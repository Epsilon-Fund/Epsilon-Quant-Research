# News-Agent Fair-Value + Calibration Showcase (hub)

> Hub backlinks: [[POLYMARKET_BRAIN]] · [[COWORK]] · [[TODO]]

## Plain-English Summary

- **The idea:** a public, non-monetized showcase of Epsilon's own news-informed fair-value probability on high-liquidity politics prediction markets, shown next to the Polymarket mid with a confidence band — with the **calibration/Brier track record over time as the hook**. A showcase of research craft, not a trading strategy.
- **Lineage:** this is the never-run Block J / LLM-forecaster frontier ([[TODO]] § future blocks). It does **not** reopen the closed financial-binary pricing results ([[od_same_day_crypto_pricing_gate_findings]], [[od_equities_index_pricing_scope_findings]]) — those cover crypto/equities binaries vs liquid underlyings, not news-driven politics fair value.
- **Discipline:** no infra before signal — the v0 gate ([[newsagent_v0_gate_preregistration]]) decides whether the view is worth displaying before any dashboard exists. Forecast ledger is append-only and anti-post-hoc (vendored `superforecasting` skill, `SF_BOOK=polymarket`), scored read-only by the first-party `calibrate` skill.

**v3.4 (2026-08-25, fifth attended session — the accuracy story becomes a number, and a less flattering one).** The forward ledger is at n=4, so the public accuracy claim was still mostly a promise. This round builds the missing number a different way: a **simulated-live RECONSTRUCTION** — replay the current model over **44 already-resolved markets** (the 40-market v3.1 backfill plus the four settled July markets, folded in via a new `--add-settled` stage), lookahead-free, on the unchanged 4-day snapshot grid, and score it against a readout **pre-registered before any number existed**. Kept permanently separate from the forward sf ledger: different object, labelled "reconstruction" everywhere, never written to the ledger, never summed with it. **Results: pooled Brier 0.1895 / log-loss 0.6359 on a 29.5% base rate — and calibration is REJECTED (Spiegelhalter Z +3.40, p 0.001).** The model forecasts 15.2% where 29.5% happens; every populated reliability bin sits above the diagonal; Murphy resolution (0.0355) is small against uncertainty (0.2082), so most of the pooled score is the base rate rather than skill. **The cadence question is answered empirically and the answer is negative: the Brier-vs-staleness curve is FLAT** (balanced set: 0.157 at 1 day vs 0.157 at 29 days, no trend), because the reconstructed FV moves a mean of only **3.35pp** off its prior and 66% of markets never move 1pp at all — prior-only scores 0.1920 against the model's 0.1895. **Cadence is not the binding constraint; the evidence channel's ability to move the number is.** Per tract, the uncomfortable answer to "where does this work": news-driven n=34 **0.2228**, poll-driven n=6 0.0779, data-driven n=4 0.0741 — worst on the markets the method is built for, though base rates differ and much of that is composition. Two declared deviations are recorded rather than buried: the family cap does not govern the four settled markets (iran ×7, fed ×5 now), and their prior is the **ledger prior of record** from 2026-07-05 rather than a fresh backfill prior, because the only forecaster in the session had already read the v3.3 settlement table and its canary would honestly answer "known". **The honest refit** folds those four in (355 pairs/49 markets → **379/53**): **α 2.85 → 2.75, which is noise** (the surface is flat to four decimals from 2.40 to 3.00, and 2.85 scores identically on the new sample), and **band_mult 0.5 → 0.75, which is a degradation** — no knob on the grid reaches nominal band coverage any more (tops out at 0.6). α was deliberately NOT refit on reach composition (no reach item has ever entered a fitted packet) nor for the data channel (DC-4). Everything is bounded by one caveat, stated on the page: reconstruction sees only timestamped-archive channels (Guardian + Wikipedia-CE + GDELT), so **RSS/newsletters/PDFs/reach are absent — an evidence-poorer world than live** — and it is **in-sample for α**, making its Brier an upper bound. **Dashboard v3.4** ships the reconstruction as § 07 (staleness curve as centrepiece, in a visually distinct dashed panel below the forward § 06), the per-tract split, the reach reality in one line (two domains reached; ukmto.org navigation-only; the dateline path is T+1), and the September Fed card gains the **§ 5 regime split with the holding row marked "WORSE than doing nothing"** plus an explicit line that today's FV equals the structural anchor because no news cleared the threshold. **Three real bugs found and fixed in the live data channel**, all the same class — quiet failure in a window nobody had run in: FRED's US-Central clock made the channel unrefreshable for hours every day, the resulting clamp poisoned the on-disk vintage cache permanently, and a failed refresh silently overwrote a good `p_struct` snapshot with an empty one. 700 tests green (17 new), headless-Chrome verified, **not deployed**. **v3.5 (2026-08-26, sixth attended session — a presentation pass, and it changes what the page admits).** No model, ledger or parameter change: the 24 published numbers are byte-identical to v3.4 and the re-render asserts that they are. The grid is now **grouped by the evidence that actually reached each market's packets** over a declared trailing window, computed from the stored packets themselves (`newsagent/provenance.py`): **news+data 1 · news, well-fed 6 · news, thin 7 · prior-only 10**. The thresholds are declared in code with their rationale and are display bands that never enter the model — a **30-day** window (the observatory is attended, snapshots have been up to 50 days apart, and 30 days is the shortest span containing at least one published packet per market at this cadence) and **≥5 distinct relevant articles** for "well-fed", which is not a new number but `DIVERGENCE_NREL_MIN` reused so the page has *one* evidence bar rather than two. Over 30 days that bar is deliberately low, and the note says so: it means the loop was reading something, not that the question is well researched. **The grouping's own finding: 10 of 24 published numbers have zero relevant evidence behind them.** Each group shows its market count and, where ≥1 market in it has settled, its own forward Brier — **well-fed 0.0347 (n=2)** against **thin 0.1241 (n=2)**, each rendered with its n attached and marked "a description of these markets, not a result", because at n=2 that is all it is. **Every card now states its provenance in words** — which channels contributed (Guardian / RSS / Wikipedia-CE / newsletters / PDFs / reach-document / reach-transcript / data-channel, plus GDELT *only* when a burst could actually have amplified, which today is 0 of 24 markets), the relevant-vs-read counts over the window, and on a prior-only card the explicit line **"no relevant evidence found; this number is the onboarding prior, unchanged since `<date>`"**. Eight of the ten prior-only markets are exactly their prior; the other two (Netanyahu −2.7pp, Putin +0.1pp) are the prior **plus decayed carry** from evidence that has aged out of the window, and they get different words rather than a false "unchanged". Prior-only cards are visually distinct — dashed muted ring, muted numeral, dashed tile, a "prior only · no evidence" chip — reusing the dashed device § 07 already uses for "this is not what it looks like". **The mechanism finding is now on the page rather than buried in a note:** every card carries a compact **prior → published, in pp** indicator (a data-channel market splits it three ways: prior → structural anchor → published, so the data channel's move is not miscredited to news), and § 07 opens with the one-line summary — across 44 reconstructed markets the FV sits a mean of **3.35pp** from its prior and **66%** never move 1pp, and **on the 24 live markets the same measurement reads 3.77pp with 58% still within 1pp**.
