# MM L2 Reconstruction Audit — Can We Rebuild the Book Cleanly?

> Hub: [[strat_market_making]] · [[POLYMARKET_BRAIN]] · Method spec: [[mm_clob_capture_semantics]] § Required Reconstruction Audit · Build plan: [[2026-06-23_mm_engine_phase01_buildplan]] · Reconciliation context: [[mm_join1_reconciliation_findings]]
>
> **Provenance:** generated by `scripts/mm_reconstruction_audit.py` (Alvaro's lane) on real VPS Parquet (**2026-06-29, esports + politics_negrisk, hours 10–13**), then **corrected during JOIN 1** with the ordering diagnosis below. The script's raw verdict ("FAIL") is **superseded** by the corrected verdict here. **Independently re-run 2026-06-30** (skeptic pass): the raw table below reproduces to the stated precision, and the same-ms-first reconstruction (98.87% esports / 99.14% politics) and bba-vs-`price_change` `timestamp_ms`-ASOF agreement (99.12% / 99.43%) reproduce the corrected ~99% — see [[mm_join1_reconciliation_findings]] § Independent verification.

## Plain-English Summary

- **What this is.** The go/no-go gate for the MM backtester: replay captured public L2 (`book` + `price_change`) through the engine's `BookTracker` and check the reconstructed top-of-book against the exchange's `best_bid_ask` checksum.
- **Corrected verdict: PASS — the book reconstructs faithfully (~99%).** The script's raw output looks like a FAIL (**29.61% clean / 68.40% mismatch** overall), but that is an **intra-millisecond event-ordering artifact**, not a reconstruction error. Once same-millisecond `book`/`price_change` updates are applied before the `best_bid_ask` checkpoint, reconstructed L1 matches native L1 **98.8% (esports) / 99.1–99.6% (politics)**. Independently, our reconstructed top matches the feed's *own* `price_change` inside-quote **99.6–99.9%** of the time.
- **Why the raw number looks bad.** In fast-oscillating books, the `best_bid_ask` telemetry **frame** is recorded with a receive clock that *precedes its own triggering `price_change` frame*. The audit orders events by `(timestamp_ms, received_ns, …)` — receive clock before event type — so the checkpoint is scored one update early. It is market-specific: a stable esports book scores **98.3% even under the raw ordering**; the fastest-churning token drops to ~23%.
- **Does it affect fills? No.** `best_bid_ask` is telemetry-only; it never mutates the executable book, and the queue model's trade-vs-cancel netting is order-invariant within a timestamp ([[mm_queue_model_audit_fixes_findings]]).

## The ordering diagnosis (four cross-checks, busiest tokens)

| diagnostic | esports | politics | isolates |
|---|---|---|---|
| recon top == native bba (raw audit ordering) | ~23% | ~34% | the "mismatch" headline |
| **recon top == feed `price_change` inside** | **99.8%** | **99.6%** | **book build is faithful → YES** |
| native bba == `price_change` inside (SQL ASOF) | 99.0% | 99.4% | the two telemetry streams are in sync |
| **recon == bba, same-ms book/pc applied first** | **98.8%** | **99.1–99.6%** | **true reconstruction accuracy** |

**Mechanism.** Same `timestamp_ms`, but the BBA frame's `received_ns` < its triggering `price_change` frame's `received_ns`. Sorting by `received_ns` before event-type checks the BBA against a book that is one same-ms update stale. Apply the same-ms `price_change` first → ~99% clean. This is the "sub-millisecond same-timestamp bursts flagged ambiguous, not errors" the build plan describes, surfacing at high frequency in fast in-play markets.

**Honest residual.** ~99% is not 100%: ~1.2% of fresh checkpoints still differ even with same-ms updates applied first, and the artifact is side-asymmetric (the ask side mismatches more than the bid under raw ordering). That residual is the genuinely-ambiguous same-millisecond burst — small, not a reconstruction bug, but not zero. Read the verdict as "~99% clean on decisive checkpoints," not "perfect."

## Decision and next step

- **Gate: PASS.** Reconstruction is faithful (~99%); proceed to use the book builder for fill simulation. The clean subset that drives fill/queue claims is far larger than the raw 30% suggests.
- **Metric fix (data-quality lane):** make the audit (and the engine's `EngineResult.l1_crosscheck`) apply same-ms `book`/`price_change` before the `best_bid_ask` checkpoint, or treat same-ms BBAs as ambiguous, so the metric stops understating reconstruction quality on fast markets. Until then, read `l1_crosscheck.both_match_frac` (~0.23–0.34 on fast markets) with this caveat.
- **Caveat (unchanged from the script):** no capture-gap signal is loaded in the standalone audit (`capture_gaps` is not part of the Parquet pull), and only L1 is checksummed (deeper levels assumed-correct-if-L1-is-correct). Gap handling on the Parquet path is verified separately in [[mm_join1_reconciliation_findings]] Check B.2.
