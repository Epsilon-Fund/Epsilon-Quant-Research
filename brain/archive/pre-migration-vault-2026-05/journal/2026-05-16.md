## polymarket-copytrade
You're looking for whether **price discovery genuinely continues** below your detection point, or whether the bid side just **gaps down** while the ask stays put. Open a weather market that's currently in the 24h pre-resolution window. Steps:

### What to look at

**On the depth chart:**

- **Tight bid-ask spread** (≤2¢, both sides clustering near a midprice): genuine consensus, track-down is at least mechanically possible.
- **Wide spread** (best bid say 0.45, best ask 0.65): one-sided market. "Track-down" here just means you'd be the lonely bidder at the bid level — your "cheap fill" would be against a one-sided dump, not at fair value. The `bid_nf_price` we observe in data is exactly this kind of print.

**On the trade tape (recent fills):**

- **Smooth descent** — prints at 0.80 → 0.78 → 0.75 → 0.70 → 0.60 over a few minutes, each step within a couple cents: real track-down. The book is rebuilding as price falls.
- **Jump-then-gap** — print at 0.80, then nothing for 5+ minutes, then a print at 0.20: that's the wide-spread scenario. Our `bid_nf = 0.20` observation is one of these. Your bot tracking down to 0.20 would only fill there if there's a seller AT 0.20 in that moment.
### The live-look diagnostic — concrete checklist

1. **Find a market currently inside its 24h window** — pick one whose resolution is between 12-24 hrs away. The bot's universe slug families are `highest-temperature-in-CITY-*` and `lowest-temperature-in-CITY-*`.
    
2. **Wait for a cross**. Watch a token whose price is approaching a round number like 0.80 or 0.70. When a trade prints at or above the level, that's a cross.
    
3. **At cross+0 seconds, record:**
    
    - best_bid, best_ask (= the spread snapshot at detection)
    - count of resting bids in the book between best_bid and cross level
4. **Watch the next 5 minutes:**
    
    - Does the best_bid stay near the cross level (sticky-friendly book — your bid at cap fills first if anyone sells)?
    - Or does it drift away from the cross level while ask stays put (wide-spread regime — track-down impossible to exploit cleanly)?
    - Time the first print on the bid side after the cross: how long? At what price?
5. **Classification:**
    
    - Best_bid stays within 2-3¢ of cross level → market is in track-down-feasible regime
    - Best_bid drifts to ≥5¢ below cross → wide-spread / sticky-only regime
    - Best_bid drops fast & ask follows within 2¢ → real crash (rare in weather)

A few minutes of this on 3-4 active markets should tell you which regime dominates your tradable universe. My prediction from the data: the wide-spread regime dominates for most weather markets — that's why the §4 `entry/opp_dir` slip median = +1¢ (median is fine) but the p90 = +6¢ (tails are wide).

Even faster: a one-pass historical diagnostic on the data we already have — classify each cross by `(p_in − bid_nf) > 5¢`. That's the proxy share of "wide-spread events." Want me to add that as a one-liner before the zip?
