**Reading STRATEGY_REFERENCE through:** subtle gotchas to flag for future-justin —
- `_default_score` is **not** Sharpe maximisation. Weighted composite (50% Sharpe + 30% Calmar + 20% return, all clipped to [0,1] with hand-tuned `*_MAX` caps).
- Scoring-side Calmar inside `_default_score` is **not annualised** (`total_return / |max_drawdown|`). Reporting Calmar is annualised. Two different numbers; don't quote the scoring one as Calmar.
- Sharpe is MODERATE trust (RF=0, includes flat bars — for low-turnover strategies that depresses both num and denom; interpret comparatively).
- `profit_factor` returns `np.inf` for all-winner samples — poisons downstream means; clip if aggregating.
- WF has **no explicit embargo** — leak boundary is the `burnin_bars` warmup-then-trim pattern. CPCV adds `purge_bars=1` at boundaries only.
- Default CPCV `N=8, k=2` → 28 splits → 105 paths → `N_eff = 7` (the CI helpers do the path-sharing adjustment).
- ≥10-20 trials per free param and "cap iteration cycles at 3-4" are **not enforced in code** — discipline guidelines, track manually.
