## polymarket-copytrade

**Major framework pivot — cohort-based copy-trading on ice, replaced by per-trader copy-execution audits.** This is the most consequential rethink since the project started.

**What changed:**
- Phase 5 cohort backtest framework is on ice. Stage 1.5 ran but results untrustworthy under cohort framing (it was solving the wrong unit-of-analysis question).
- Replaced with per-leader audit: `scripts/domah_copy_audit.py`, ~90s per leader, produces (family × role × hour) deployable-cell map per leader. 7 leaders audited so far; 1-6 deployable cells each.
- **`phantom_position_score` retired as primary arb filter.** Replaced by fill-concentration metrics in new `traders_directionality.parquet`. Pool C (NegRisk specialists) was 91% contaminated under old phantom-score definition. The metric still has signal but isn't authoritative anymore.
- **New metric `split_position_signature`** catches `splitPosition`-based directional construction. 10 of top-50 traders by lifetime PnL fail this filter — architecturally uncopyable, no execution improvement can fix it. `0xd38b71f3` confirmed as a member of this class (the "atypical taker, role_balance=0.22" framing was a *consequence* of split-construction, not a strategy choice).

**Empirical findings (n=7 audits):**
- Highest lifetime PnL does NOT predict copyability (Spearman ρ = −0.21). Contradicts the cohort framework's implicit ranking.
- Recency is the strongest predictor: `active_days_last_90d` ρ = +0.81.
- Hold-to-resolution helps: ρ = +0.64. Reinforces the 96.4%-held-to-resolution finding.
- High `style_role_balance` HURTS copyability: ρ = −0.64. Maker-conviction leaders are adversely selected if you copy them as makers — your maker order tends to fill when their thesis is wrong.
- NegRisk political directional bettors are weakly copyable on the taker leg, uncopyable as makers.

**Copyability is per-(trader × family × role × hour), not per-cohort.** Cross-leader signal is weak — only 2 narrow shared cells across 7 audits. The cohort framing assumed leaders within a cohort would have transferable copy-behaviour; the audits show they don't.

**6 new methodology lessons captured** in `methodology-lessons.md` 2026-05-16 block:
1. Copyability is per-(trader × family × role × hour), not per-cohort
2. Highest lifetime PnL does NOT predict copyability
3. Recency dominates copyability prediction
4. Maker-style traders are adversely-selected if copied as makers
5. Architectural uncopyability via splitPosition (10 of top-50 fail this)
6. `phantom_position_score` retired as primary arb filter
