**Findings worth surfacing** (from reading formulas.md cover-to-cover):

1. **96.4% of closed positions held to resolution** (`is_held_to_resolution`, §1.5: 260,381,355 / 269,974,929). Massive implication for exec: leader-sells-X-bot-sells-X mirror-exit only fires on 3.6% of positions. The other 96% just resolve in-place via redemption — RTDS doesn't emit anything for redemption. Exec needs an explicit resolution-handler path or it'll appear permanently stuck holding. Flagged in TODO; deserves its own line item in `01-execution.md` open-questions.
2. **`max_abs_drift = $0.0000`** between `pos_total_pnl` and `mkt_total_pnl` across 2.58M rows (D.2). Exact dollar agreement, not just rounding-tolerable. The collapse is a regrouping, not a recompute.
3. **`mkt_sharpe` p99 = 11.17, max = 107.20** (vs `pos_sharpe` p99 = 14.90, max = 1.66×10¹⁵). Cohort guards (`mkt_std_pnl > 1.0 AND n_pos > 200 AND active_days > 90`) keep the published pools clean (D.3 confirms), but Sharpe is fundamentally misspecified at the trader level — periods aren't time-uniform. Use `mkt_profit_factor` and `mkt_dollar_win_rate` for ranking; Sharpe stays diagnostic.
4. **Cohort pools already materialised.** `data/cohorts/*.parquet`, six files, 14,053 union rows. **947 addresses qualify for 3+ pools, max overlap = 4** — by design the pools span orthogonal dimensions. The 947 cohort-robust set is the natural candidate list for due-diligence.
5. **`raw_trades` lags real-time by ~9 days** (Goldsky indexer). Latest fill 2026-04-24; today is 2026-05-10. Walk-forward backtests need test cutoff ≥ 9 days behind run date.
