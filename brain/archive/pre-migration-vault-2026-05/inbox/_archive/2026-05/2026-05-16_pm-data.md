# 2026-05-16 — pm-data

Scope: polymarket data / cohort research. **Major framework pivot** — cohort-based copy-trading research has been put on ice; replaced by per-trader copy-execution audits. Captured 2 days after report date (today is 2026-05-18). Backfill-ingest.

- **`phantom_position_score` retired as an arb filter.** Replaced by fill-concentration metrics in new `traders_directionality.parquet`. **Pool C was 91% contaminated under the old phantom-based definition.** This is a major retraction of how arb-detection was framed.
- **New trader-level metric: `split_position_signature`.** Catches `splitPosition`-based directional construction (the `0xd38b71f3` failure mode). 10 of top-50 traders by lifetime PnL fail this filter — they're structurally non-copyable because their position-construction path isn't an `OrderFilled` event.
- **`0xd38b71f3` confirmed architecturally uncopyable** — not a "taker-heavy unusual profile" as previously framed in `04-cohort-pools.md`. The taker-heavy role balance was a *consequence* of splitPosition construction, not a strategy choice.

## 2. Decisions taken

- **Copyability is per-(trader × family × role × hour), not per-cohort.** Cross-leader signal at this granularity is weak: only 2 narrow shared cells across 7 audits. The cohort framing assumed leaders within a cohort would have transferable copy-behaviour; the audits show they don't.
- **Cohort signal investigation deferred** until ≥12 audits available. Cross-leader intersection at 7 audits is too thin to discriminate signal vs noise.
- **Stage 1.5 rebuild deferred** until per-leader audit framework matures.
- **Threshold-setting for layer-1 metrics deferred** — needs ~15-20 audits before thresholds are calibrated against meaningful sample.

## 3. Bugs / surprising lessons

- **`phantom_position_score` was contaminating Pool C 91%.** The metric framed `1.0 = clean directional, >1.0 = arb-shaped`; empirically (as flagged in last week's calibration note) all six pools sat at 1.4-1.8 baseline. The contamination found here is the next layer down — for the NegRisk-specialists pool specifically, 91% of qualifying traders were arb-flavoured by the new fill-concentration metric. **Lesson:** when an arb-detection metric flags <100% of arb-shaped traders, it's not "tunable" — it's structurally missing a class of arb construction.

- **Highest lifetime PnL does NOT predict copyability** (n=7, Spearman ρ = −0.21). This contradicts the implicit framing of `04-cohort-pools.md` (which ranked candidates partly by PnL). The cohort framework had no concept of "deployable cell density per leader" — the right metric for picking who to follow.

- **Recency dominates copyability prediction.** `active_days_last_90d` is the single strongest predictor (ρ = +0.81). 90-day-recent leaders are far more copyable than lifetime-PnL champions. **Lesson:** when copying execution, lifetime metrics are deceptive — recency captures the current liquidity/style regime.

- **Hold-to-resolution helps copyability** (ρ = +0.64). Reinforces the earlier `is_held_to_resolution = 96.4%` finding and the polymarket exec-side "leader sells X% → bot sells X%" rarely-fires problem. Leaders who hold to resolution are more copyable because their exits are mechanical (redemption), not contested (queue priority).

- **High `style_role_balance` HURTS copyability** (ρ = −0.64). Maker-conviction traders are *adversely selected* when copied as makers — your maker order tends to get filled when their thesis is wrong (someone else is paying for the privilege). Confirms the ADR 0001 `pricing_mode_recommended` heuristic was inverted: high role_balance ≠ `leader_fill` is better. **Lesson:** the leader's natural execution role isn't always your best execution role when copying.

- **NegRisk political directional bettors are weakly copyable on the taker leg, uncopyable as makers.** Confirmed via Domah + negrisk_directional_1. The "NegRisk specialists" cohort framing in `04-cohort-pools.md` is too coarse — copyability depends on which leg you take, not which pool the leader is in.

- **Architectural uncopyability is a real category.** `0xd38b71f3` constructs positions via `splitPosition` (CTF-level operation, not an `OrderFilled` event). This isn't a "they trade fast and we can't keep up" problem — it's "their position state changes outside of any event we can subscribe to." **No execution improvement can fix this.** 10 of top-50 traders by lifetime PnL fall into this category. The new `split_position_signature` filter catches them.

## 4. Open questions / blockers

- **Cohort signal investigation** — the 2 shared cells (`other / taker / afternoons`) across 7 audits could be real or noise. Needs ≥12 audits to discriminate. Until then, don't design a cohort-level signal.
- **Live-test calibration of queue priority on Polymarket WS** — still the bottleneck for the WS-passive weather strategy (carried over from 2026-05-15). Also relevant now: the per-leader audit framework predicts deployable cells under an *assumed* execution model. Same execution-model-as-first-class lesson applies.
- **Stage 1.5 rebuild** — premature until audit framework matures (~15-20 audits).
- **Threshold-setting for layer-1 metrics** — needs more audits before calibrating.
